# anxin-simple-daily-redesign - desktop

**Preview file:** designs/previews/anxin-simple-daily-redesign-desktop.png
**Spec SSOT:** designs/specs/anxin-simple-daily-redesign.md

## Layout

Desktop presentation canvas, 1440 x 900. Place three tall phone mockups on a soft `#F6FAF8` background. The first phone is the daily first-entry page and should be the focal screen. The second phone is the simplified home after today's declaration. The third phone is the simplified items list. Keep large quiet whitespace and avoid a marketing hero layout.

## Modules

- Daily status phone: app name, `今日未申报` chip, title `今天先确认一次`, explanatory sentence, one status strip, primary CTA `我今天平安`, secondary action `先看预案`.
- Home phone: compact banner `今日已申报 09:12`, title `安心托付`, readiness summary, next action, icon bottom nav.
- Items phone: title `事项`, circular plus button, three simple item rows with status labels, icon bottom nav.
- Token rail: tiny swatches for background, surface, primary, confirm, review, text.

## Visual Details

Use final tokens from the SSOT: background `#F6FAF8`, surface `#FFFFFF`, soft panel `#EAF4F1`, primary `#0A6B63`, confirm `#2F8A67`, review `#9A6A2D`, text `#173B37`, muted `#667B76`, border `#D8E7E2`. Use system sans typography, generous line height, 20 to 24 px screen padding, 8 px card radius, and understated borders. The signature element is the `今日申报条`, not a seal or document stack.
